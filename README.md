## 👋 Hi there, I'm Imran

A T-shaped **AI & Data Science** professional with **10+ years** of experience developing and productizing robust, scalable AI models and services. Born and raised in Dhaka, Bangladesh 🇧🇩, now based in **Singapore** 🇸🇬.

- 🔭 **Senior AI Scientist** at [**Aon**](https://www.aon.com/), building LLM, VLM and document-intelligence solutions
- 🧪 Researching **multimodal AI**, **agentic AI** and **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**
- 🎓 **M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence** (Distinction), University of Liverpool, with **Dissertation of the Year 2025**
- 📝 **30 publications** and **500+ citations**, including a NeurIPS 2024 workshop paper
- ✍️ I write about data and ML on [my blog](https://imranabdullah.com) and [Medium](https://medium.com/@abdalimran)
- 📫 Reach me at **abdalimran@gmail.com**
